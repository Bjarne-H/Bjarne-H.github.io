Our Story · Edward J. O’Reilly

From the Bronx to the World Trade Center.

Edward J. O’Reilly grew up in the Bronx in a large Irish family. After studying at Manhattan College and SUNY Albany, working in public service and helping build the city’s tunnels as a Sandhog, an opportunity brought him to California — first into international shipping, then into the law. Twenty years later, that real-world experience still shapes how he approaches every client’s case.

  1. The Bronx · Family

    A Bronx Upbringing

    Edward grew up in the Bronx in a large Irish family, where his father served as a police officer. It was a household that valued hard work, straight talk and looking out for the neighbors — values he still practices by.

  2. New York · Public Service

    Manhattan College, Albany & City Hall

    Educated at Manhattan College and SUNY Albany, Edward began in public service — advocating for tenants at the New York City Department of Housing Preservation & Development and working for a member of the New York State Assembly on housing, labor and legislative matters.

  3. Underground · Sandhog

    600 Feet Below the City

    As a New York City “Sandhog,” Edward helped build the water and subway tunnels that run hundreds of feet beneath the streets. It was hard, dangerous work, and it taught him what working people are up against when something goes wrong on the job.

  4. New York · A Change of Plans

    Heading West

    Edward attended classes at the NYPD Academy and logged training runs through the Bronx — until a major opportunity changed his plans and brought him west to California.

  5. California · Global Trade

    Inside International Shipping

    In California, Edward worked inside international shipping with American President Lines (APL) and Orient Overseas Container Line (OOCL) — the commercial and transportation systems behind today’s maritime and logistics disputes — and also worked as a private investigator before turning to the law.

  6. Long Beach · 2006 – Present

    Twenty Years at 1 World Trade Center

    After earning his law degree at Pacific Coast University School of Law, Edward was admitted to the State Bar of California and the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California. Since 2006 the firm has practiced from Suite 800 at 1 World Trade Center, backed by a team and a network of associated attorneys across California and nationwide.
