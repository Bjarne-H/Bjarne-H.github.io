Featured Practice

Partition Actions

When co-owners cannot agree on what to do with a property — siblings who inherited a home, former partners, or investors on title together — California law lets any co-owner ask the court to divide the property or order it sold and the proceeds split (Code of Civil Procedure § 872.010 et seq.). We represent owners on either side: compelling a sale, negotiating a buyout, or protecting your share and the credit you are owed for mortgage, taxes and improvements.

  • Inherited & family property
  • Partition by sale or in kind
  • Co-owner buyouts & settlements
  • Accounting for payments & improvements
  • Heirs’ property protections
  • Title, lis pendens & referee sales

Edward J. O’Reilly comes from a long line of police officers. He followed them as far as the NYPD Academy before an opportunity brought him to California, where he built a career as a private investigator and then as an attorney. A cop’s instincts, an investigator’s discipline and a litigator’s preparation give him a practical edge in uncovering facts, checking backgrounds and taking on powerful opposing parties.

  1. The Bronx · Family

    A Family That Wore the Badge

    Edward grew up in the Bronx in a large Irish family with deep roots in law enforcement. His father was a police officer, and so were more uncles, cousins and in-laws than anyone at the dinner table could easily count. He was raised around people whose job was to show up, get the facts straight and protect their neighbors.

  2. New York · Public Service

    Manhattan College, Albany & City Hall

    Educated at Manhattan College and SUNY Albany, Edward began in public service — advocating for tenants at the New York City Department of Housing Preservation & Development and working for a member of the New York State Assembly on housing, labor and legislative matters.

  3. Underground · Sandhog

    600 Feet Below the City

    As a New York City “Sandhog,” Edward helped build the water and subway tunnels that run hundreds of feet beneath the streets. It was hard, dangerous work, and it taught him what working people are up against when something goes wrong on the job.

  4. NYPD Academy · The Bronx

    Following His Father’s Footsteps

    Like the generation before him, Edward set out to become a New York City police officer. He attended classes at the NYPD Academy and logged training runs through the streets of the Bronx — until a major opportunity changed his plans and brought him west to California.

  5. California · Investigator

    Private Investigator & Global Trade

    In California, Edward built an extensive career as a private investigator — locating witnesses, running background checks and documenting the facts other people miss — while also working inside international shipping with American President Lines (APL) and Orient Overseas Container Line (OOCL).

  6. Long Beach · 2006 – Present

    Twenty Years at 1 World Trade Center

    After earning his law degree at Pacific Coast University School of Law, Edward was admitted to the State Bar of California and the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California. Since 2006 the firm has practiced from Suite 800 at 1 World Trade Center, backed by a team and a network of associated attorneys across California and nationwide. Edward is a member of the Los Angeles Police Emerald Society.
